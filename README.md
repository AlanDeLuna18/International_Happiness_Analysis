# Analyzing and Predicting Migration Trends By Country

#### Background
Happiness is something influences our daily lives way more than we think. It affects everything from our motivation to even our own health. Is it often observed by experts that each country has its own happiness rates, some differing very heavily from the other. Of course, these large differences is because some countries have a better quality of life than others, leading the general population to feel more at peace with their well-being. However, what's the most influential factor that influences these rates? 


#### Purpose
- Learn and understand the patterns that happier countries tend to have 
- Pinpoint the strongest factors that influence the happiness in a given country 

#### How Will This Be Done? 
- Consider significant aspects of a country such as GDP and use k-means clustering to group countries togther based on these characteristics
- Visualize and analyze these trends and see what that tells us about world wide happiness
